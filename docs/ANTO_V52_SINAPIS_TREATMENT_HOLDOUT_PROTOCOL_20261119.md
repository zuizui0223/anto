## New cross-treatment holdout (three folds, not old shelter holdout)
- Hold out all original plants from one whole rainfall treatment across all five shelters. Train on plants from the other two original treatments; repeat for Control, Drought, PrecipRed. Every eligible source plant scored once.
- The SDEA v17 original categorical treatment indicators are NOT usable to predict an unseen, absent treatment level; thus v52 models deliberately exclude treatment dummy variables from ALL fitted models. This is a new treatment-blind predictive family, NOT simply original v17 models replayed in a new fold.
- Source outcome is original authors' estimated contemporaneous seeds-per-plant transformed by log1p, not daughter recruitment or genetically transmitted fitness.
- U0 = unpenalized intercept plus training-standardized log1p total source flower supply.
- U1 = U0 plus training-standardized mean flower display size and mean petal width.
- U2 = U1 plus training-standardized log1p sum of original visitor interactions / each plant's original single observation time in hours.
- U3 = U0 plus the identical observed visit-rate predictor without morphology (secondary).
- Exactly fixed ridge alpha 4 on slopes, not intercept; train-only normalization, no target outcome leakage, no hyperparameter or outcome-driven model selection.
- Report signed MSE(U1) minus MSE(U2) separately for each of the 3 heldout original treatments (PRIMARY visit increment), mean across treatments, plant-weighted mean, and 2 secondary contrasts U0→U1 and U0→U3. Retain all absolute baseline/model MSEs; no pooling with Ulex fruit fraction, Phlox fruit counts, or SDEA v17 shelter-fold MSE.

## Original stratum-constrained observational placebo (NOT a causal randomization test)
After exact source admission, run 200 fixed pseudo-permutations (seed 20261119), shuffling ONLY the recorded plant visit-rate predictor among source plants within each fixed original shelter×rainfall-treatment stratum. Preserve each plant's flower traits, seed estimate, shelter, treatment, observed source group membership and the original three leave-treatment-out folds; do not independently shuffle visitor taxa as observations. Refit U2 and compute equal-treatment U1-minus-U2 gain each repeat; report median/q025/q975/min/max and count >= actual. Not an ecological p-value, because original visit rates were not experimentally assigned.

Fixed descriptive gate for cross-treatment observed-visitor information: original U1-minus-U2 (1) mean gain strictly positive equal-treatment and plant-weighted, (2) positive for at least 2 of 3 treatment folds, and (3) greater than the placebo reference 97.5th percentile. If any fails, label CROSS_TREATMENT_VISITOR_TRANSFER_NOT_ESTABLISHED; do not silently switch to U3, smaller subset, different response or tuning. Passing implies only within-one-trial descriptive context transfer, never causal ecological mediation or broad interspecific transfer.

## Review ceiling
