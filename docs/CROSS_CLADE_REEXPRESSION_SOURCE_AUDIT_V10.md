# ANTO v10 — independent-plant-clade source audit for the lost-phenotype / latent-capacity question

**2026-10-19 JST.** This is a **retrospective, outcome-visible source audit**, not a newly conducted global meta-analysis, independent prospective replication, joint functional assay, or inference of a universal plant pigment-regain mechanism. This audit intentionally includes real **negative and partial mechanisms**, rather than counting every flower-colour paper as successful evolutionary reversal.

## Separating five kinds of evidence

- **P0 visible history only:** flower hue differences + tree/geography; historical polarity may remain unresolved.
- **P1 retained structural components:** intact pigment-pathway coding regions; not proof of floral regulatory capacity.
- **P2 mechanistic but not naturally regained:** genetic manipulation / expression perturbation demonstrates a route for pigmentation or loss, but not a historical return within the lineage.
- **P3 evolutionary regain inferred + biochemical/expression context:** comparative trait mapping infers an evolutionary gain from an unpigmented ancestor; identity and functional causation not experimentally settled.
- **P4 molecularly documented regain:** a dated/phylogenetic return is supported by causal genetic changes restoring floral pigmentation in the relevant natural lineage. Ancestral uncertainty and interspecies gene flow still require attention.

P0–P4 are *evidence classes*, not a universal hierarchy where any P4 paper by itself proves a universal recovery-window hazard. Molecularly demonstrable restoration does not itself identify its long-run reproductive establishment probability.

## Data actually accessible and what remains missing

- Verified externally findable raw molecular data: Ho & Smith 2016 Dryad **10.5061/dryad.49rs8**, published six files: Chi, Dfr and F3h sequence text files plus their source-readme files. This permits a carefully source-pinned **intact coding / within-Iochrominae constraint** reanalysis; it does **not** by itself provide joint same-individual germline competence, seasonal developmental activation, temporal population recovery, ecological fitness or an independent clade test.
- Larter 2018 provides species-level comparative colour/pigment and seven-gene expression summaries; actual supplementary file access and exact same-specimen linkage remain to be audited before any row-wise merge with Ho & Smith 2016.
- Mimulus and Antirrhinum provide experimentally characterized regulatory *controls* with distinct units and outcome definitions.
- Cirsium sampling plans and individual-level molecular evidence remain under aza3. ANTO does not fetch or reinterpret private sample information as a positive result.

## The useful biological cross-clade observation right now

Two **different** forms of hidden developmental potential have credible published support in more than one clade:

1. **Coding conservation despite floral absence**: Iochrominae losses preserve assessed structural pathway sequences even when floral pigment is absent.
2. **Regulatory routes for regain or modulation**: Petunia has documented natural floral regain through distinct MYB routes; Mimulus and Antirrhinum provide different experimentally documented ways to shut down or release pigmentation.

This is **mechanistic diversity and an existence proof of some retrievable pathways**, not a numerically estimated probability that a random lost trait can return, not a comparative test of common re-expression clocks, and not evidence that any specific Ryukyu lineage historically regained anthocyanin.

## Decisive next scientific test (pre-outcome recommendation, not yet performed)

**Target:** Does direct measurement of retained floral regulators, structural/enzymatic modules and historical introgression reduce (or fail to reduce) the residual predictive value of ancestral lineage and colonization histories for future verified phenotype re-expression?

The minimal comparison is anto v9 M1 vs M2/M3, but **only** when the molecular observations are independent of the outcome and when at least two additional clades beyond Petunia and Cirsium have complete eligibility. Cross-validated clade-heldout models must use distinct true population/lineage units and matched assays. For publications without a dated regain direction and true functional toolkit, retain P0–P2 roles and do not code the response event as 1 or 0.

**Critical negative outcome:** if all external data remain P0–P3, the conclusion is EMPIRICAL_CROSS_CLADE_RECOVERY_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE, not NO_TRAIT_REVERSAL.

## Relationship to model v10

ANTO v10 studies an idealized three-streak versus first reappearance frontier using exact a,r,d,g transitions. The source audit is **not calibration of any a,r,d,g from the published papers**, and no Petunia/Iochrominae event is entered as one independent simulation draw. Forni et al. 2026 ([Biological Reviews](https://doi.org/10.1002/brv.70168)) already explain broad reversibility; the missing scientific advance is validated **quantitative persistence/closure conditional on hidden competence**, not repeating that pathway genes can remain intact.
