# ANTO v10b — Why a one-off floral induction experiment cannot identify evolutionary recoverability

**Date:** 2026-10-19. **Type:** exact conditional counterexamples and source-compatible experimental design. **No new biological material, verified regeneration, fitness or cross-clade evolutionary result.**

## A falsifiable advance over merely observing colour regain

*Visibly lost but internally competent* and *visibly lost and molecularly incapacitated* flowers can be indistinguishable in any number of photographs before an induction. Even a single successful colour-induction experiment can fail to distinguish the number of lineage resources from the propensity of those resources to activate. And a measured baseline functional resource state plus one induction does not identify the probability that a phenotype stays expressed.

This is a mechanistic-identification statement **within the declared v10 finite model**, not a new theorem about hidden-state Markov chains or a discovered universal property of plants.

## Witness 1 — identical first induction, divergent repeated expression

Two hypothetical cohorts consist of independent biological lineages, all initially visibly trait-negative.

| World | Initially jointly competent theta | Induction a | Continuation r | Irrecoverable joint-resource loss d | Acquisition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A — competence rich / weak continuation | 1/4 | 2/5 | 1/10 | 1/10 | 0 |
| B — competence poor / strong continuation | 1/8 | 4/5 | 4/5 | 1/10 | 0 |

**Exact observational equivalence at generation 1**: P(expressed) = theta*a = **1/10** in BOTH worlds. P(white)=9/10. The complete first-induction Bernoulli data law is identical across these two hypothetical worlds regardless of how many genuinely independent lineages are measured. Therefore the optimal equal-prior model-classification risk remains **1/2** for any number of *first-pulse-only* independent samples.

Yet the probabilities of **at least one 3-consecutive-generation positive run by generation 12** are different:
- A = 0.004569530427 (~0.457%).
- B = 0.084593651136 (~8.459%).

This is a rank difference in **eventual repeated expression**, not a proof of a difference in seed fitness, genetic fixation or permanent establishment. It is a constructed example, not a rate fitted to Petunia, Cirsium or Iochrominae.

### Counterfactual opportunity under 40 independent lineages (NOT the 40 existing C. sieboldii field targets)

A simple binary three-streak response in **40 truly independent hypothetical lineages** has distinct binomial count laws with probabilities pA and pB above. For equal prior chances of A/B and an optimally chosen classifying rule, the exact risk is

R_40 = (1/2) Σ_(k=0)^40 min[ BinomPMF(40,k,pA), BinomPMF(40,k,pB) ]
     ≈ **0.07570**.

For 10/20/40/80 independent lineages, the risks are about 0.22897 / 0.12912 / 0.07570 / 0.01804. These are **optimal discrimination errors for two fully specified models**, not statistical power for an unknown ecological effect, not a design guarantee under nuisance/rate uncertainty, and NOT 40 independent evolutionary origins in the current aza3 sample plan. First-only samples have R=0.5 even if n goes to infinity in this pair.

A proper biological experiment must establish the lineage-level independence, repeated tracking, stage-consistent induction mechanism and endpoint. Flower and photo replicates from one plant do not increase the biological n.

## Witness 2 — even a complete baseline gene assay and one induction do not estimate continued expression

Fix BOTH theta=1/8 and activation=2/5 in two worlds. Fix degradation=1/10. Only the conditional repeat-probability differs (r=1/10 versus r=4/5). Both baseline molecular census and first positive fraction are then EXACTLY identical: theta=1/8; first induction P=1/20. However the three-run event differs widely at 12 generations.

Thus **measuring intact coding genes or joint functional modules is necessary for the resource question but is not sufficient to estimate the ecological continuation operator**. Longitudinal expression and reproductive-function evidence must be separately acquired.

## Witness 3 — even infinite preinduction white-flower photographs and perfect first/pairwise module statistics can fail

Use the two v7 finite joint-inventory examples:
- even parity: marginal module presence 1/2 each; every pair 1/4; triple resource P(111)=0;
- odd parity: SAME marginals/pairs; triple resource P(111)=1/4.

Keep activation=0 and structural degradation=0 throughout an uninduced period. Any finite number of observation steps is **all white** for both worlds, and the first/pairwise module moments agree. At a later *identical* a=1/2 induction:
- even parity: positive fraction **0**;
- odd parity: positive fraction **1/8**.

This is a sharp synthetic identification counterexample linking v7's higher-order joint-module uncertainty to v10's prospective expression. It does not imply real modules recombine freely or that a published Petunia or Iochrominae ancestor had one of these parity distributions.

## What the user-observable field data can actually distinguish

### Current published evidence

- Petunia: published molecularly documented floral pigment regulatory restoration examples; these are one genus, not two independent clades.
- Iochrominae: Ho & Smith (2016, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12862-016-0675-3) found intact Chi/F3h/Dfr coding regions in pigmented and unpigmented lineages; Larter et al. (2018, https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msy117) found conservation of upstream expression and differential downstream expression, with an inferred regain in I. edule. These are **published cross-species comparisons within one clade**, not new direct longitudinal experimental recoverability probabilities.
- Ryukyu Cirsium: aza3 currently has young-leaf RNA and literature-source homolog analyses, NOT same-plant floral enzyme activity, genotyped ancestor-to-descendant return or validated time to closure.

The publicly discoverable Ho & Smith Dryad dataset https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.49rs8 contains Chi/Dfr/F3h alignments. This audit confirmed that such a dataset exists but **did not obtain and independently rerun its raw sequence files**. The Dryad API can provide metadata, but raw-file ingestion needs an accessible download authorization/environment; no fabricated sequence-ratio estimate is introduced.

### Decisive prospective study under aza3 empirical ownership

At the same **individual plant / known population / clade**, without inventing new specimen counts or declaring field permits:

- **C1 confirmed visible absence**: calibrated floret organ/ontogenetic stage, anthocyanin HPLC/LC-MS (not algorithmic RGB white by itself);
- **C2 confirmed retained functional inventory**: nuclear locus/genome and allele genealogy; structural DFR/ANS and paralog controls; same-flower regulatory expression, organ-specific pathway use; all required components measured *jointly* rather than independently across plants;
- **C3 controlled ability to re-express**: matched experimental conditions and stage-specific controls; reversible physiological induction distinguished from heritable expression change; positive/negative pathway controls;
- **C4 repeated persistence**: descendant segregation, propagule stage, flower anthocyanin expression in more than one generation, differentiation from maternal/epigenetic transient induction and immigrant pollen;
- **C5 realized ecological fitness**: reproductive function, filled viable achenes, independent relevant ecological interaction/selection manipulations.

Design outcome categories: PHENOTYPE_ONLY, STRUCTURE_ONLY, REGULATORY_CAPACITY_SUPPORTED, INTRINSIC_REEXPRESSION_SUPPORTED, IMMIGRATION/INTROGRESSION, HERITABLE_REPEAT_CONFIRMED, FITNESS_CONFIRMED, NOT_IDENTIFIABLE. These categories are not a monotonic tree: e.g. imported alleles can regenerate function without intrinsically reactivating the original genome.

**Critical distinction:** no existing planned 40 C. sieboldii individuals count as forty independent whole-clade regain events. The Phase-A 298-target acquisition plan is unchanged and presently uncollected. If only leaf RNA/homology is available, the correct result is **UNMEASURED_FUNCTIONAL_MODULE**, not pathway retention or failure to regain.

## Publication consequence

Do not title a paper "Lost traits can return"; trait reversals are long-established. The genuinely testable cross-clade advance is whether **measured joint functional competence (C2) and independently measured repeated expression/fitness (C4/C5) explain future return better than phenotype/ancestry alone** and how its ecological parameters vary between clades and habitats.

Under current public evidence this remains a **proposed empirical hypothesis**, not supported biological generality. A stronger-than-methods high-tier paper requires independently admitted non-Petunia clades and biologically different whole-population heldout tests. Negative source qualification is not a biological null.
