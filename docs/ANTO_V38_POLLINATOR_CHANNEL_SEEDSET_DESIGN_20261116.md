# ANTO v38 — pollinator access, effective service, and reproductive output are different gates

**2026-11-16 | source-verified published-study design frozen before new cross-study arithmetic.** Transparent continuation of ANTO v37, not additional wet-lab work or a single integrated data set.

## Main falsifiable question

When flower colour is changed through AN2, can a plant gain an additional visitor guild without losing the established guild? Does more attraction automatically improve effective pollination and seed production? Distinguish:
A. genetic edit/introgression → changed display;
B. specific pollinator *first choice / visit* under fixed background;
C. actual floral reproductive-organ contact and pollen transfer;
D. fruit/capsule formation or seed production;
E. intergenerational heritable maintenance of the gene/color trait in a natural population.

**Primary observability question:** Is A→B→C→D→E experimentally observed for the same phenotype-changing AN2 Petunia intervention and same biological units? If the original published programs are not so joined, state **NOT_IDENTIFIED_SAME_TREATMENT_END_TO_END** regardless of positive individual stages. Never fill missing stages using observations from different genotypes or studies.

## Source programs, predeclared study grains and observations

1. **Hoballah et al. (2007), Plant Cell** DOI [10.1105/tpc.106.048694](https://doi.org/10.1105/tpc.106.048694), original full text https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1867374/ . Existing ANTO v37 source: functional AN2 introduced into white *P. axillaris*; greenhouse first choice *Manduca sexta* white=30 versus coloured=6 (n=36, exact two-sided binomial P=0.000069601694...). Bee landings favour coloured line (author original tests). **Separate wild populations:** at José Ignacio and Carmelo in Uruguay, native **non-transgenic** *P. axillaris* had capsule formation under both day-only and night-only pollination access; authors report Mann–Whitney Z=−0.711 P=0.477 and Z=−1.38 P=0.167, respectively, n=4 observational blocks/replications per locality (see original Figure 2C). **These non-significant contrasts do not prove day/night service equivalence, and field capsules are NOT the AN2-colour manipulation's fitness response.** Wild *P. integrifolia* nocturnal-only treatment produced zero capsules (original Fig 2D, n=4). No transgenic-vs-control seed set measured.
2. **Dell'Olivo & Kuhlemeier (2013), Evolution** DOI [10.1111/evo.12178](https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.12178), original full text https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/evo.12178 . Reciprocal **AN2 near-isogenic** introgressions in two **different genetic backgrounds**:
   - *P. axillaris* natural white and AN2+ coloured (white→coloured): *Manduca* first choice coloured=15, white=14 (n=29, author χ² P=0.85268); no detected directional preference of established moths. Bumblebees prefer AN2+ coloured (n=4 *hives*, author Wilcoxon P=0.001), and native wild solitary bees also visit AN2+ more (n=7 observation days, P=0.001). Do not claim old guild is proven equivalent by nonsignificance.
   - *P. inflata* natural purple and AN2− white (purple→white): *Manduca* first choice white=15, purple=3 (n=18, author χ² P=0.005); wild bees do not significantly discriminate between purple and white (n=7 days, author P=0.277; greenhouse bumblebees n=4 hives P=0.525).
   - These are **four genotype conditions within one original study**, not four independent radiations. Source reports no measured stigma pollen deposition, fruit/seed production of these two AN2 NIL pairs. Authors propose **reduced specialization and potential reproductive assurance**, not a directly observed seed-set advantage. Relative projected corolla area of AN2− P. inflata is 28% smaller than parent; do NOT presume all floral characters identical.
3. **Brandenburg, Kuhlemeier & Bshary (2012), Current Biology** DOI [10.1016/j.cub.2012.06.058](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2012.06.058), original abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22840518/ . Separate low-nectar genetic introgression in *P. axillaris* (~one-third of wild-type nectar; **NOT colour AN2**). Hawkmoths do not otherwise discriminate between low-nectar and wild-type in first attraction, **but shorten probing time on low-nectar flowers, and seed production is lower**. Hand-pollinated low-nectar flowers have higher reproductive potential; nectar supplementation restores moth-mediated seed production near hand-pollinated levels. This demonstrates behavior during service can be more informative about seed output than attraction alone. Original abstract reports direction, NOT a source-verified paired seed-count/fold effect; no invented numeric effect sizes or P values.
4. **Rodrigues et al. (2018), AoB PLANTS** DOI [10.1093/aobpla/ply057](https://doi.org/10.1093/aobpla/ply057), https://academic.oup.com/aobpla/article/10/5/ply057/5113022 . Field-observed pink *P. secreta*, independently of Esfeld 2018's ancestry AN2 reconstruction but in the **same genus/radiation**: **38 observed Pseudagapostemon sp. visits**; 22 visits of that genus were documented landing on or engaging reproductive parts, with potential stigma contact while collecting pollen. Recorded visits are **NOT 38 distinct plants nor 22 quantified successful pollen transfers**. This study did not perform an AN2 allele-restoration contrast nor directly measure the fruit/seed output attributable to restored AN2 in the observed population. This is an important natural contextual observation, not a direct causal "natural regain→fitness" chain.

Optional original surrounding context, not an independent new contrast: Dell'Olivo et al. (2011) isolation barriers DOI 10.1111/j.1558-5646.2011.01279.x; seed-derived plants 1914/290 with no hybrids, but family structure/detection incomplete and no intervention-specific AN2 introgression fitness. Do not treat as four new Petunia radiations or convert no hybrid events into binomial ancestry fitness.

## Frozen calculations and negative gates

- Recompute **two-sided exact binomial** first-choice P values for original 2007 n=36 (white30, colour6) and 2013 n=29 (colour15, white14) / n=18 (AN2−white15, purple3) under simple 1/2 first-choice probability. These are **post-publication arithmetic checks, not raw study statistical reanalyses**; 2013 authors report χ² tests, so do not demand identical P values.
- Source-independent fixed stage table per **experimental program**: `gene_manipulated`, `first_choice`, `body_contact_or_pollen`, `capsule/seed`, `multi_generation`, and `same_treatment_link_to_next`. Explicitly distinguish *natural wild plant* observation in 2007 from AN2-transgenic observation in same paper. Numeric unit provenance: 36 moth choice events, n=4 field replications/site, 29 and 18 moth choices, 4 hives, 7 field days, 38 bee visits/22 observed reproductive contacts; none independent lineages.
- **Positive evidence**: AN2 introgression may *expand visitor options* without detectable disfavor from established pollinators in the tested backgrounds; wild white *P. axillaris* can receive effective day and night pollination as measured by capsule formation; nectar/behavior mediation can alter seed set without detectable attraction difference.
- **Negative/failure**: no source contains AN2-colour change + quantified pollen transport + same-treatment seed set + heritable frequency trajectory. Do not infer genetic resilience, natural selection coefficient, lifetime population growth or a universal channel-continuity probability.
- Treat a nonsignificant choice contrast as **not demonstrated difference**, not equivalence proof. Treat P=0.477 and 0.167 as "no detected field day/night difference with limited replication", not evidence that both guilds have exactly the same effectiveness. Treat 2007 + 2013 repeated Petunia as two source designs, not two independent phylogenetic radiations.
- Separate 2012 **nectar-manipulated seed output** from AN2-colour-mediated seed output. Original published no-seed-count access: no numeric seed effect estimate is permitted. Pseudagapostemon 22/38 is a field observation fraction within visits, not successful seed or pollen-deposition probability.
