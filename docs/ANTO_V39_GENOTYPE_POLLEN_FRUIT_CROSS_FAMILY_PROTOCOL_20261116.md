# ANTO v39 — Does floral phenotype predict pollen delivery and realized offspring fitness?

**2026-11-16 — prereadout audit and source-first quantitative replay plan.** This is a new ORIGINAL independent research-program analysis across different angiosperm families; the prior v38 Petunia records are immutable. Pooled effect sizes are prohibited until outcomes and experimental contrasts are actually comparable. Observations below are original author-reported unless a separately authenticated raw Dryad source replay is clearly indicated.

## Falsifiable biological question

Flower colour may influence reproductive fitness via (A) pollinator choice, (B) pollen acquisition/export, (C) compatibility of pollen with maternal genotype, (D) seeds/fruit, and (E) descendant genotype frequencies. **Can each step be identified for the same intervention in a single original study?** What happens when an additional reproductive gate is altered but flower colour is only an experimental marker?

### Precommitted four original-source programs

1. **Jones & Reithel (2001)**, *American Journal of Botany* 88:447–454, DOI **10.2307/2657109**, original [Wiley/AJB](https://bsapubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2307/2657109), [PubMed 11250822](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11250822/). *Antirrhinum majus* controlled outdoor arrays; two heritable colour morphs of one-locus genetic architecture; freely foraging *Bombus appositus* and *B. flavifrons*. Original 1994 Barr and 1995 Avery arrays measured visit frequency/duration, pollen receipt/export and dye transfer; **different 1997 Rosy and Barr arrays** jointly measured visitation and offspring allele/genotype composition. There is **no single original plant/cross/year with all three visit→pollen measurement→offspring outcomes**. The 1997 Rosy array received no nectar supplementation; in the 1997 Barr array the yellow flowers were supplemented, so the two array comparisons are not a colour-only controlled genetic intervention. Receipt related most to visit number, export to visit duration; observed assortative foraging aligns with offspring genotype deviations. **No observed natural transgenerational fixation, no floral colour genome-editing / experimental ancestral regain, and no raw array CSV yet admitted.** Botanical current family Plantaginaceae; study used historical Scrophulariaceae.
2. **Hopkins & Rausher (2012)**, *Science* DOI **10.1126/science.1215198**, original [PubMed 22300852](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22300852/). *Phlox drummondii* with nearby *P. cuspidata*: 2 colour loci (flavonoid F3′5′H hue and R2R3-MYB intensity), common-garden field contrast across sympatry/allopatry plus interspecific pollinator transitions and hybridization. Authors report reinforcing selection favouring higher pigment intensity in sympatry, not detected allopatry, compatible with reducing hybrid matings. **This is an allele/fitness-context study, not an anthocyanin loss/regain or identical Petunia AN2 seed-set experiment.** No raw individual maternal/paternal data retrieved in this gate; previous Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.8792d carries *different 2011/2012 population microsatellite study*, NOT these 2012 fitness observations.
3. **Campbell et al. (2013)**, *Functional Ecology* DOI **10.1111/1365-2435.12131**, original [Wiley](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2435.12131). *Wahlenbergia albomarginata*, Campanulaceae, whole-ramet pollen addition and reduction, reflectance-measured floral pallor and seed output. Original **43% greater seeds after supplemental pollen** (mean treatment difference), yet selection favouring whiter flowers becomes **stronger** under added pollen, inconsistent with pollen-receipt-only mediation and consistent with non-pollination mechanisms affecting maximal seed production (mechanism not identified). **Important negative mechanism**, not proof color genes cause resource use; no directly genotyped floral locus and no joint floral gene editing.
4. **Burgin, Lewis & Hopkins (2026)**, *PNAS* **10.1073/pnas.2525299123** (peer reviewed, supersedes 2025 bioRxiv DOI 10.1101/2025.06.17.660178). Original [PMC12933113](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12933113/), original [Dryad 10.5061/dryad.zgmsbccr3](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi%3A10.5061/dryad.zgmsbccr3). *Phlox drummondii* Polemoniaceae: source original small CSVs `vigs_fruitset.csv`, `vigs_pollentubes.csv`, `vigs_qpcr.csv`, optionally `diallelcross_fruitdata.csv` and `diallelcross_pollentubedata.csv`. Original RNA silencing uses **DFR** to visibly bleach petals as a **reporter**, with and without co-silencing the S-specific pistil identity receptor **PdPIRK**; outcome fruit set after controlled crosses and stained pollen-tube number. **Flower colour is NOT the PdPIRK causal SI gene.** Validate row-level outcomes separated by virus vector, allele matching, original pistil IDs and pollen IDs. Do not conflate the 2025 bioRxiv and final 2026 PNAS as two studies.

Predeclared descriptive analyses:
- Counts and fruit fractions within `TRV2-vector × Treatment × Flower_Color × Pistil_Genotype × Pollen_Genotype`, with explicit unique `Pistil_ID × Flower_Number` and controls for repeated plant.
- **Colour marker only:** DFR-only VIGS suppresses corolla pigment; contrast `white/silenced` vs `pigmented/unsilenced` by original plant/pollen compatibility in the DFR-only vector. An observed zero/non-difference in fruit is not proof of equivalence.
- **PdPIRK-mediated compatibility:** for vector targeting S1 or S4 in pistil, compare source reproductive compatibility for pollen matching that allele versus nonmatching allele and DFR-only controls (where original tests provide observations). Keep a **per-pistil-plant paired mean change** and its positive/negative count, and raw-group observation denominators; no pseudoreplicate significance or unconditional causal colour effect.

- **No same-study free-foraging pollinator choice** in this 2026 controlled-cross experiment; fruit set is **compatibility under manually applied pollen**, not a response to colour by pollinators.
- Code's primary inferential focus: identify whether the increased pollen tubes/fruit are attributable to PdPIRK allele-specific silencing rather than simply a white petal. This is a **mechanistic reproductive gate independent of visible flower-colour label**, not natural evolutionary regain of AN2 colour, pollinator service, or genotype persistence.

### Cross-study measurement ladder and gate

Register four independent original **papers** across three current families: Plantaginaceae (Antirrhinum), Polemoniaceae (Phlox 2012+2026, shared genus not independent lineage), Campanulaceae (Wahlenbergia). Count original programs as four, **not four independent evolutionary radiations**. Define `measured_visitation`, `measured_pollen_receipt_export`, `controlled_pollen_compatibility`, `measured_fruit_seeds`, `offspring_genotype`, `natural_population_frequency_trajectory`, `regulatory_gene_direct_perturbation`, `same_treatment_all_steps`. Distinct stages across different original studies do NOT produce one causal chain or a pooled effect size. For the Antirrhinum original paper, a **visit→offspring allele-frequency** link occurs in 1997 original arrays. A separate **visit→pollen receipt/export** link was measured in 1994/1995 arrays; these should be represented as two partial original source chains, **not** a directly measured end-to-end individual chain. Source published 1997 initial→offspring sulfurea allele frequency is Rosy 0.50→0.44 and Barr 0.50→0.52; heterozygote fraction is Rosy 0.50→0.41 and Barr 0.50→0.43. Effects of flower size, nectar and potential selfing are not isolated. For Phlox 2026, **gene→pollen compatibility→fruit** is controlled laboratory crossing, DFR pigment bleaching is a separate visible reporter. For Wahlenbergia, colour–seed selection not via receipt remains an explicit counterexample.

**Prediction / falsifier:** If DFR-only white silencing and additional PdPIRK allele-targeting have indistinguishable original compatibility and fruit patterns when stratified by shared pollen genotype/plant, a simple PdPIRK-specific claim is not corroborated in this replay; preserve negative. Source manuscript's original published evidence still exists but cannot be declared independently replicated from mismatched raw data. If original CSV unavailable, the quantitative source access gate is technical and no new confirmed effect size can be claimed.
