# ANTO v41 — original 2014 natural cline, two-locus selection and epistasis

2026-11-17. EXPLORATORY source-audit after inspection of Hopkins et al 2014 and Supplement. No new field data, likelihood/MCMC fit or natural longitudinal selection trajectory.

## Source and independent study grain
Hopkins, Guerrero, Rausher & Kirkpatrick (2014) Current Biology doi:10.1016/j.cub.2014.07.027, original main https://people.duke.edu/~mrausher/Hopkins%20et%20al.%202014.pdf and supplementary https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=e97477246cc95302de8f7778f4f03b23460d04cb&repid=rep1&type=pdf. The original paper measured 12,024 adult floral phenotypes (32 populations, 5 transects) and 2,581 offspring flower colours from 382 maternal field plants; fitted a spatial 2-locus pollen-migration population model to both. Original paper's model and historical inference remain the original authors' work.

## Published original Table 1 and selection model
Four morphs: light-blue LB (iiH_), dark-blue DB (I_H_), light-red LR (iihh), dark-red DR (I_hh). Published phenotypic relative fitness each REGION normalized separately: allopatry LB=1, DB=.86, LR=.89, DR=.90; sympatry LB=.17, DB=.53, LR=.86, DR=1. Original Table1 locus coefficients: allopatry red hue h -0.11 [-0.14,-0.08], dark intensity I -0.14 [-0.17,-0.10], epistasis +0.15 [0.11,0.19]. Sympatry ancestral blue H -0.14 [-0.19,-0.09], ancestral light i -0.47 [-0.69,-0.30], epistasis -0.22 [-0.37,0]. All confidence intervals belong to authors' source fitted model.

## Fixed v41 arithmetic and falsifiers
Compute e_allopatry = 1 - DB + DR - LR = +.15, and e_sympatry = 1 - DB + LB - LR = -.22 exactly from rounded phenotype fitness. Record ratio DR/LB as .90 in allopatry, ~5.88 in sympatry; DO NOT pool independently normalized relative fitness as realized seed totals or transgenerational allele odds. Preserve hue-specific 2012 field no-detected effect versus significant hue selection in 2014 broad spatial model: these are different estimands, not an automatic contradiction. For 2014 Table S1 provisional numbers, count 32 populations, 5 transects, exact population identifiers, genotype phenotype counts, mixed-colour sites and recombinant phenotypes, but do not recompute MCMC or use dominance to invent exact allele genotypes. The 645 observation mismatch MUST remain visible and block any claim of full original 2014 survey replay. Original paper reports cline widths 4.0km light, 3.7km red, spatial dispersal variance .83 km^2, and 36 demes in each of 5 transects, NOT a 2-deme toy.

## Evidence ceiling
Original population phenotypes and mother-offspring floral phenotypes are one-year/cross-sectional material used to fit an equilibrium model. It is evidence of a spatially maintained cline under regionally different selection and ongoing modeled pollen migration, not direct multi-year natural allele fixation or evolution of the original white-to-purple AN2 restoration pathway. Source authors themselves note original spatial model cannot by itself identify selection specifically due to reinforcement rather than other sources, while other field studies support reinforcement. v40 2012 s=.323 from q=.90 and h-light=.43,h-dark=.21 is a different viable-hybrid proxy; NEVER rebrand as the 2014 spatial intensity locus s_I=-.47.
