# ANTO v35 — independent white-flower pathway-state contradictions

**2026-11-15 | External original-paper evidence map; design frozen before new evidence-state arithmetic.** This is a *published-study* source synthesis, not a newly downloaded paired HPLC/RNA dataset, species-level prediction refit, or experimental validation of ancestral trait regain.

## Question

Does the visible state **white flower** reliably identify the state of a floral anthocyanin pathway? The preceding ANTO v26–v34 Petunieae results predict *present* chemical amount and identify a severe control-positivity problem for separate phylogenetic distance effects. External evidence from plant families that are not Petunieae can challenge the *universal* inference "white => no anthocyanin pathway gene expression" and the converse "detected pathway expression => visible anthocyanin".

## Registered independent original programs and observation grains

1. **Ericaceae, cultivated Hirado azalea and relatives** — original *The Horticulture Journal* (2020), doi: **10.2503/hortj.UTD-142** (verify J-STAGE canonical DOI), source https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/hortj/89/3/89_UTD-142/_html/-char/en . 6 wild-species samples and 25 cultivar samples had HPLC pigment composition/colour measurements; four or more white-flowered cultivars, and the named old white cultivar **'Shiro-ryūkyū'**, are discussed. The authors report qRT-PCR on 6 wild samples and 18 cultivars, including white flowers, detecting F3'H, F3'5'H, DFR and ANS in white despite lack of visible anthocyanin; small anthocyanidin peaks are discussed for 'Shiro-ryūkyū'. **Cultivar samples are NOT 31 independent species; transcript detection is NOT enzyme functional rescue, and HPLC & RNA use different floral stages and need not represent the same individual.** Do not invent numerical qPCR fold changes or a full joint individual table.
2. **Convolvulaceae, *Ipomoea lacunosa*** — original 2020 *PLOS ONE*, DOI **10.1371/journal.pone.0231263**, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7153891/ . White versus purple flower-throat groups assayed with qPCR: original paper reports CHS roughly 100-fold lower, DFR greater than 100-fold lower, MYB1 roughly 100-fold lower in field-derived white plants; phenotyping/cross progenies studied separately. Field qPCR n=4 white and 4 purple, HPLC n=3 white and 3 purple for *I. lacunosa* plus 2 *I. cordatotriloba*. **Do not equate HPLC and qPCR individuals or report approximate fold descriptions as exact digitized values.** This is a regulated-low state, not proof of coding-gene destruction.
3. **Solanaceae Iochrominae and Petunieae context** — existing ANTO v23/v25/v26/v34 source receipts only. Larter et al. (2019) DOI 10.1002/dvdy.82 reports fixed-white versus polymorphic-white differences in late DFR/ANS expression but original Dryad p5dq84v RAR source bytes were NOT acquired in previous v23 access gate (401/403). Wheeler et al. (2023), DOI 10.1098/rspb.2023.0275, report some seemingly white corollas still have anthocyanins in localized veins. Both are **context/author-reported**; no new independent reanalysis or joined specimen-level expression/pigment source data is implied.

## Preregistered evidence-state evaluation

Only publish supported categorical evidence rows. Preserve three orthogonal objects: `visible_flower_class`, `anthocyanin_measurement` and `transcription_state`. Do not coerce "low expression", "detected transcript", "unknown" and "null coding function" into one Boolean. A source's species/cultivar unit must be tagged and study programs deduplicated by original DOI/source cohort, not counted by number of observations or follow-up publications.

The two independent target examples above support the narrower observation "**white floral phenotypes occur with different documented transcriptomic states in distinct original programs**". This is NOT a formal heterogeneity meta-analysis because qPCR assays, units and pigments differ, nor a causal experiment reactivating an ancestral gene. The stronger universal claim "a fully functioning ancestral pathway is retained in every white flower" is **NOT tested** and should be blocked. Paper-reported 2019 Iochrominae and 2023 Petunieae belong to supplementary context, not additional confirmed v35 paired individual observations.
