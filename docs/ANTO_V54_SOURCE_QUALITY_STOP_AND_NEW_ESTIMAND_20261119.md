- Original harvest `No_Seeds_Total` and `No_rep_units` were both present and nonnegative integer in **all 2,217** matched source rows, but `No_Aborts_Total` was **missing in 1,056 of 2,217** (47.63%). The subset of 1,161 rows where all three were numeric had original unit accounting sum difference **0**. This is not a zero abort count, and imputing missing aborts as zero would be biologically wrong.

However **4,087 of 11,682** (34.99%) violated the original assumed total vs Apis+wild arithmetic identity, with largest discrepancy **9** interaction units. Direction: Apis+wild > reported total in 1,933 rows and reported total > Apis+wild in 2,154. This was not a rounding tolerance issue.
